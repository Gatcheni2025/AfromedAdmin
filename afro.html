THE HOLDING COMPANY

Strategic integration.
Measurable community impact.

Afromed.life is the strategic holding company that unites three specialised operating entities to deliver end-to-end emergency healthcare solutions across KwaZulu-Natal. Established in 2012 and headquartered in Durban’s Avoca Hills, we combine 24/7 advanced life support operations, HWSETA-accredited professional training, and targeted community development under one values-driven group.

Our integrated model creates powerful synergies that standalone organisations cannot replicate. Graduates from our College move directly into operational roles within EMS and community programmes, while on-the-ground intelligence from our Development arm informs proactive emergency deployment and preventive health initiatives. This closed-loop approach delivers better clinical outcomes, stronger local capacity, and sustainable youth employment pathways.

Our Mission

To deliver integrated, high-quality emergency medical care, build professional healthcare capacity through accredited training, and drive sustainable community health development across KwaZulu-Natal — leaving no community behind.

Our Vision

A KwaZulu-Natal where every community has rapid access to life-saving care, skilled local responders, and proactive health support. A province where young people see healthcare as a viable, dignified career and communities are active partners in their own wellbeing.

Our Values
• Clinical Excellence & Patient Safety First
• Youth Empowerment & Local Capacity Building
• Community Trust, Cultural Respect & Dignity
• Integrity, Accountability & Transparency
• Innovation that Serves People, Not Technology

AFROMED EMS

24/7 Advanced Life Support & Event Medical Services

Headquartered in Durban with a secondary base in Manguzi (northern KZN), Afromed EMS has been a trusted provider of advanced life support and emergency medical services across KwaZulu-Natal since 2012. We operate a modern fleet of 17 specialised vehicles and maintain full membership with the Board of Healthcare Funders (BHF).

Our Story

Founded in 2012 by Sthembiso Emmanuel Ngubo and Nelisiwe Gloria Ngcobo — two young Black social entrepreneurs and professional colleagues with over 18 years combined critical care ambulance experience — Afromed EMS has grown into one of KwaZulu-Natal’s most respected private emergency medical service providers.

We operate from two strategic bases: our primary Avoca Hills headquarters serving the greater eThekwini Metro and surrounding areas 24/7, and our Manguzi branch providing critical coverage for the uMkhanyakude District and northern KZN border regions.

Our Modern Specialised Fleet

We maintain a modern, well-equipped fleet of 17 specialised vehicles operated by qualified paramedics and emergency care practitioners. All vehicles undergo rigorous maintenance and equipment checks to ensure readiness at all times.

11 × Advanced Life Support Ambulances
Fully equipped with cardiac monitors, ventilators, defibrillators, advanced airway equipment and medication. Staffed by qualified paramedics and ECPs capable of advanced interventions on scene and during transport.
6 × Rapid Response Vehicles
First-on-scene capability for rapid trauma and medical response. Equipped with ALS packs, oxygen, AEDs and communication systems. Ideal for high-density urban response and event support.
Additional patient transport vehicles and 4x4 capability available for difficult terrain and rural response. All vehicles are BHF compliant and regularly audited.

Core Clinical Services

Advanced & Basic Life Support (ALS/BLS)
On-scene stabilisation, advanced airway management, cardiac monitoring, pain management, IV therapy and safe transport to appropriate facilities.
Inter-Facility & Patient Transportation
Safe, coordinated transfers between facilities with full clinical handover. Includes high-care, ventilated and bariatric patients where required.
Trauma Care & Aftercare
Specialised response to traumatic injuries, spinal immobilisation, haemorrhage control and full continuum of care with follow-up coordination.
Community CSI & Youth Development
First aid kit distribution, school safety programmes, youth first responder training and post-incident support in partnership with local structures.

Event & Sports Medical Coverage

We provide tiered, professional medical cover for sporting events, music festivals, corporate functions, school events and large public gatherings. Our packages are customised to event size, risk profile and duration.

Bronze Package
First aid teams + basic equipment for smaller events and low-risk gatherings. Ideal for school sports and community functions.
Silver Package
ALS teams + doctor on site + dedicated treatment area. Suitable for mid-size corporate and sporting events.
Gold Package
Full medical command post + multiple ALS teams + on-site doctor/nurse + evacuation plan. For major festivals and PSL matches.
Recent clients include PSL matches, Durban Rocks festival, corporate golf days, school sports tournaments and large music events. Our teams have supported over 12,000 attendees across multi-day festivals with zero major hospitalisations in recent deployments.

HWSETA Accredited Programmes

All programmes are fully accredited by the Health and Welfare Sector Education and Training Authority (HWSETA) and nationally recognised. They combine blended theory with intensive practical simulation.

Advanced Emergency First Aid Responder HWSETA
5-day intensive
Advanced trauma assessment, airway management systems, spinal immobilisation, multi-casualty incident management and comprehensive simulated assessment. Ideal for safety officers, workplace response captains and those seeking to advance in emergency care.
Fulfills high-risk corporate compliance • Validates advanced clinical skills
Basic Emergency First Aid Responder HWSETA
2–3 days
Core life-saving techniques: CPR (adult, child, infant), choking management, wound care, burns, fractures and common medical emergencies. Meets OHSA requirements for general workplace first aiders.
Instant life-saving capacity • Core OHSA safety law compliance
Forestry SHE Representative HWSETA
Specialised Safety, Health and Environment protocols for high-hazard logging, silviculture and plantation operations. Covers forestry-specific hazards, legal requirements and practical risk mitigation.
Protects plantation operations • Mitigates complex agricultural hazards
Forestry Hazard & Risk Assessor HWSETA
Techniques to detect physical, machine-related and environmental risks in harvesting zones. Learners develop protective action plans and systemic proactive safety metrics.
Lowers heavy liability claims • Establishes proactive safety metrics
Incident Investigator Course (NQF aligned) HWSETA
Scientific investigation methodologies to identify root causes of workplace incidents, analyse data and prepare legally sound documentation suitable for audits and regulatory processes.
Prevents recurrence • Maintains legal integrity during audits
Early Childhood Practitioner + Paediatric First Aid HWSETA
Child psychology fundamentals, physical development milestones, nutritional planning and specific paediatric first aid response. Supports crèche and preschool licensing compliance.
High professional growth • Ensures crèche/preschool licensing compliance
New Venture Creation (NQF 4) HWSETA
Foundational business accounting, legal frameworks, marketing strategies and operational planning for health, safety and first aid entrepreneurs. Ideal for those wanting to start their own training or safety consultancy.
Translates practical skills into viable businesses • Builds financial literacy

Admissions & Enrolment Process

1
Submit Online Application
Complete the form on our website or contact our registrar. Provide ID, highest qualification and any relevant prior experience.
2
Assessment & Interview
Shortlisted applicants complete a basic numeracy/literacy assessment and a short interview (in-person or virtual) to ensure programme fit.
3
Offer & Registration
Successful applicants receive an offer letter with programme dates, fees and payment options. Secure your place with a deposit.
4
Orientation & Commencement
Attend orientation (includes app setup, uniform/kit collection if applicable) and begin your structured learning journey.
Fees & Funding

Programme fees vary by duration and complexity. We offer flexible payment plans, HWSETA grant guidance where applicable, and limited bursary support for deserving youth from partner communities. Corporate clients may sponsor groups for in-house or customised training.
