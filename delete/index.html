Account & Data Deletion Request

Please use this form to request the permanent deletion of your Afromed account and all associated personal data. This process is irreversible.

Warning: Once your request is processed, all of your data, including your phone number, emergency contacts, and location history, will be permanently removed from our servers. You will no longer be able to use the Afromed service with this account, and this action cannot be undone.

This must be the phone number you used to register for the Afromed app.

After you send the request via email, our team will process it within 7-14 business days. We may contact you at the phone number provided to verify ownership of the account before proceeding. You will receive a final confirmation once the deletion is complete.

If you have any issues, please contact us directly at Admin@afromed.co.za.
